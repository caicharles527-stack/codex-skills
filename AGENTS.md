- Explicit subject names or Skill requests take priority over topic overlap. If Methods versus Specialist is unclear, use the Task Sheet; ask only if still material.

- Preserve the existing Academic Humanizer; do not recreate or overwrite it. For SACE work involving the scholarly editing described above, also read it. Subject accuracy and assessment requirements govern subject content; language editing must preserve the student's voice.
- Preserve valid wording and reasoning. Explain important corrections. Never fabricate data, research, citations, writing history, or claims of bypassing AI detection.
- Missing Task Sheets or Rubrics permit provisional checks only; label gaps and do not claim rubric-based grading without the actual criteria.
